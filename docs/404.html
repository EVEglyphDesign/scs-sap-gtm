Steel Cloud Solutions would sit next to the SAP North America presales team as a Decision Intelligence Center of Excellence. Not a reseller, not an SI, not a displacement of anyone already at the account. A processor delivering into the trust already between the two of you.

What SCS does

Two motions, both additive:

  • Client-side readiness. Deployed at prospects and existing SAP accounts to help them get their data straight — aligned, understood, and ready — so that when the SAP work happens, it lands on a real foundation.
  • Presales intelligence. At accounts where the SI is struggling to reposition, SCS reads the situation and hands the account rep a sharper picture than they currently have. What they do with it is their call.

The speed that matters

A position that used to take a month now takes a couple of days. That is the input presales actually needs — early, defensible, and specific to the account.

Why this protects SAP, not just SCS

The real strategic risk in enterprise AI today is customers lifting the semantic model out of SAP and porting it straight into a hyperscaler. Once that move is made, SAP's long-term position at that account is materially weakened.

SCS is SAP-first by default and fluent in Microsoft, Google, and AWS deployments. That combination lets us represent SAP's semantic capabilities firmly inside the customer's AI conversation — keeping the model where it belongs while the customer still gets the AI answer they are asking for.

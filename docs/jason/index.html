The Datasphere adoption lane.

One challenge, three sizes — where SCS can help.

The lane where an arm's-length advisor materially moves the number for SAP North America presales is Datasphere adoption. Not the presales conversion problem, and not the account team's engagement structure — those are yours. The specific gap SCS can carry is getting customers to actually use Datasphere, and to see it as strategic rather than as a BW replacement.

Three sizes of the same challenge

Micro — getting people to use it at all

BW is on the retirement path. Analytics for Office — SAP's Excel add-in — moves with it. A very large share of SAP finance and operations users still live in Excel every day. Datasphere is the successor SAP is pointing them at, and it is not landing on its own. Customers need a hand getting the first real model up, the first real report in front of a user, the first real Excel-replacement moment. That is a legwork problem, and it is exactly what SCS can front-end.

Mid-cap — the feasibility trap

Mid-cap SAP customers do not own the advanced pieces of the portfolio. They cannot easily reach for the alternatives that a large enterprise could substitute. That leaves them feeling stuck between an ageing BW estate and a Datasphere step they are not staffed to take. In that segment, Datasphere is often the only feasible bridge — and an advisor who can take them across it is the difference between the account moving forward and the account drifting.

Strategic — the keystone of Business Data Cloud

Once the customer's data lands in Datasphere, the semantic model stays inside SAP. That is the door to SAC and to Databricks with SAP still controlling access and meaning. Datasphere is the piece that lets SAP keep ownership of the intelligent-enterprise ground floor while the customer still gets the analytics and AI options they want on top. Every account that adopts it is an account SAP does not lose to a hyperscaler lift-out.

Three service shapes

All three services run inside the customer's four walls, with the customer's private data. No external data movement, no privacy trade, no reseller paper. The services are sized so a first account can start with A, add B when the decision is on the table, and go to C only when the customer is committed to a transformation.

Service A — Datasphere technical support and product demonstration

SCS goes into the customer's non-productive systems and stands up the Datasphere functionality the customer would be getting on the licence. Typically we point a test system at production so the customer's SMEs get a working preview against their own real data — configuration, connection, first semantic model, first live report — ahead of the buying decision.

For your team, this replaces synthetic decks and vendor demos with something the customer's own SMEs can already touch inside their perimeter, without the account team or an SI carrying the burden of a synthetic proof.

Service B — Business-case build for the SAP decision

A directed decision-support engagement scoped to whatever SAP decision the customer is working through. SCS goes in with our structured tools and works backwards from that decision to the numbers that support it — process, financial, and operational context, gathered inside the customer's four walls and structured into the economic justification the customer's own executives can carry into their approval.

This is decision support, not audit. We are not pulling entitlement, we are not grading adoption, we are not measuring anything for SAP. We are helping the customer get the numbers they need, quicker, to make a purchase they are already leaning toward.

Service C — Executive decision-intelligence assessment

For accounts on a major SAP transformation, the full structured piece: a published, highly respected industry advisor signs the recommendations, and SCS runs the working surface underneath.

SCS draws the advisor from a network of senior SAP subject-matter experts who have worked together for decades and trust each other; we find the person among us who is most appropriate to lead the assessment for that account and industry. Behind the signature, our team runs data gathering, session hosting, recording, structured capture, blueprint drafting, and repository custody — all inside the customer's four walls. The output is one operational blueprint tying the customer's real transaction and process data to the transformation reasoning the SI and SAP are already discussing.

Why this is a safe call for you

  • No pressure on your conversion numbers — that is your team's problem to solve, and we are not proposing to solve it for you.
  • No SI displacement. SCS supports adoption behind the account team; it does not reposition against a partner already on paper.
  • No audit posture. Service B works forward from the SAP decision the customer is already considering, not backward from an entitlement review the customer would hear as a threat.
  • No obligation created on your side of the line. If it stops fitting, it unwinds cleanly — no vendor entanglement, no reseller paper.
  • Additive to the accounts where the SI is strong; a lifeline in the accounts where the SI is not.
  • Jeff runs the commercial relationship afterwards. Sourcing, quality, the offshore bench, the way work lands back at the account.
